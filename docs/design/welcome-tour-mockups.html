Welcome tour and first-time tips

What someone new sees after the opening screen: five swipeable cards, each one picture and one line, with Skip on every card. After that, small tips appear the first time someone reaches a feature. Mark each card and tip Keep, Change or Drop, and edit any words in the note. Nothing is built until you've answered.

The pictures here are simple drawings standing in for real screenshots of the app, which the built tour will use (never F1's photos). The cards are drawn on the open inner screen; in laptop pose and on the closed screen the picture sits above the words.
