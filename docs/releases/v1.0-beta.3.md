Beta 3 brings the race data to the picture: a track map, the timing tower, driver cards and race
marks that follow what you're watching and never run ahead of it.

**On a replay**

- **Track map** as a floating panel or built into the layout, with flags, the safety car and a
  −1 s / +1 s nudge if it drifts from the picture.
- **Tap a car** on the map or the tower to bring up its onboard in one of the small feeds.
- **Timing tower and driver cards** with lap times, sectors, tyres, gaps and speed.
- **Timeline marks** for lights out, the chequered flag and safety-car, virtual-safety-car and
  red-flag periods, up to the moment you're watching ("Show the whole race" in Settings shows
  them all).

**For the fold**

- **Director's Desk** (flat): the big picture, the other feeds, the tower and the map.
- **Race Engineer** (half-open like a laptop): an onboard above, the engineer's console below,
  with that car's readouts, lap times and the cars around it.
- **Scrub Deck** (laptop): the video above, a touch timeline of the race below. Drag to move
  every feed, and tap a mark to jump to it.

**Races**

- **Pit circle:** the lap drawn as a circle, with each car placed by its gap to the leader. Tap
  or hold a car to see where it would come out after a stop.
- **Battle** chip in races when two cars are close.

**Live sessions** (first try on the Las Vegas weekend, November 19–22)

- F1's live timing for the map, tower, cards and console, with one shared connection.
- The session is recorded on your phone so you can rewind. If you join late, the earlier part
  fills in from F1's archive. Until it does, that part says "Timing catching up".
- When another live programme starts, it's offered with "Switch" or "Not now".

**Fixed on the real iPhone Duo (November 18–19)**

- Live sessions play: F1 TV protects live video with Apple's FairPlay, and the app now gets the
  keys the way F1 TV's own web player does (checked live at the warm-up and FP1).
- Picking a driver's onboard on a live session page plays that driver, live (checked at FP1).
- Races whose main feed is INTERNATIONAL (such as Abu Dhabi 2021) play again.
- Multiview without multiview for a race (older races, or live for now) offers "Watch the main
  feed".
- "From the start" / "Watch live" points at the button you tapped; rows of tiles keep the same
  height; closed, the feed uses the outer screen's full width.
- Errors say why ("Couldn't load F1 TV" ends with F1 TV's own error number and words).
- Settings › About shows which code a build came from.

**Privacy:** live timing goes only to F1's timing server with your own F1 TV sign-in. Circuit
outlines come from MultiViewer without anything about you. Recordings and car data stay on your
phone and are deleted after 7 days.

**Still to check by hand.** It runs on the real iPhone Duo and played live at FP1, but the
changelog's "Untested — check later" lists for builds 11 to 16 still name what hasn't been tried.
Live multiview (several live feeds at once) isn't in this release: live plays one feed at a time.
