Beta 4 brings team radio and a closed phone that is worth looking at: radio you can read and hear
as the race goes, buzzes for what happens, and a closed screen that tells you when to open it.
Everything follows the picture and never runs ahead of it.

**Team radio**

- **Radio panel** beside the map: each clip under its lap, shown only once the picture has
  reached it. Tap a clip to hear it while the feeds go quiet. Marks for clips on the Scrub Deck,
  and the last three clips in Race Engineer's console.
- **Transcripts:** each clip turned into text on the phone by iOS's own speech recognition,
  shown in quotes under the clip once the picture reaches it. No server and no microphone.

**The closed phone**

- **Three pages on the outer screen:** Picture (two feeds stacked, or one with the timing
  tower), Radio Scanner (one driver's clips, which play by themselves as they happen) and Gap
  Thermometer (two cars, the gap in big digits, the colour going from blue to red as it closes).
  Swipe up or down between pages.
- **Unfold to See:** a flag, your driver's stop, radio, a pass or a retirement slides in as a
  card. Open the phone and every feed jumps to just before it, with "Back to where you were" to
  return.

**Haptics**

- One feel for each yellow flag, safety car, virtual safety car, red flag and chequered flag, and
  for your driver's stop and purple sectors, felt as the picture reaches them. Settings › Haptics
  has a switch and "Feel it" for each.

**In book pose**

- **Onboard and Logbook:** one onboard on the left, and that driver's radio, laps, stops and race
  control on the right.
- **Ear-Matched Rivals:** two onboards, one per screen, each driver's radio in that ear with
  headphones.
- **Hinge mixer** (Settings › Team radio, off by default): while a clip plays, folding gives more
  radio and opening gives more commentary.

**Live multiview:** a live session's multiview now plays: each feed is asked for on its own, the
way Safari asks, so each comes as FairPlay. A live tile that stops asks again with a fresh link,
at most once a minute. Checked only at a race weekend.

**The single-feed player** has the app's own controls instead of Apple's: play and pause, ±10 s,
LIVE, speed, one menu for commentary language and subtitles, mute, picture in picture, AirPlay,
and play and pause from the lock screen.

**Polish:** a new opening screen ("Night circuit") and Apple's design pass: the white selected
tab, Apple's glass buttons and more. Two design-pass items (q8 and q9) wait until the laptop-pose
bugs are fixed.

**Privacy:** clips come from F1's own archive and are turned into text on your phone only. They
are kept on the phone for 7 days and then deleted. Nothing is sent to a speech service. iOS may
download its own speech model the first time.

**What to check on the phone first**

1. Closing the single-feed player (and tapping a car, then closing) doesn't crash, and leaves
   nothing on the lock screen or in Control Centre.
2. Team radio on a replay: clips appear only as the picture reaches them and play with the feeds
   quiet.
3. Settings › Debug › "Transcribe a test clip": run it once and send back what it shows.
4. Closed: swipe the three pages; open the phone with a card up and check the feeds jump.
5. Haptics: "Feel it" for each, and a few in a replay.
6. Book pose: Onboard and Logbook, and Rivals with headphones.
7. T-R1: play an onboard and listen to its team-radio audio track. Is it continuous radio, clips
   only, or onboard sound?

**Known limits**

- The hinge mixer works on radio clips only until owner check T-R1 is answered. It works in the
  multiview only; the single-feed player still silences the feeds under a clip.
- "Transcribe a test clip" is still in Settings › Debug. It stays until the owner has run it, and
  is removed after that.
- A live feed that keeps failing asks again once a minute (one more request each time), until it
  plays or you remove it.
- Never tried on the phone yet: the app changes were checked on a computer only. The changelog's
  "Untested — check later (all of beta.4)" lists what hasn't been tried.
- Haptics may stop with the screen off or the app in the background; they start again with the
  next one after you come back.
